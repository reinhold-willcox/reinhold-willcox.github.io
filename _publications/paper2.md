excerpt: 'Non-conservative binary mass transfer requires some treatment of the angular momentum loss of non-accreted matter. In rapid population synthesis, the available models are approximate, and do not self-consistently treat the angular momentum loss with the response of the donor star to mass loss. In this study, I quantify the impact of ignoring this problem on the fraction of interacting binary systems which ultimately experience a common envelope or not.'
